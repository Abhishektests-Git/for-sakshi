# For Sakshi ♡

A birthday website made with love — a single-page celebration with a love letter, a blow-out-the-candle wish, a timeline of our story, a slideshow of our moments, and a 3D orbiting sphere of 154 photos in the Our memories section.

## Running it

It's a plain static site — no build step. Just serve the folder with any static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, or a local server) and open `index.html`.

- `index.html` — the whole page (markup, styles and scripts)
- `assets/` — photos, the orbiting-sphere photos (`orbit-1.webp` … `orbit-154.webp`) and the birthday song
